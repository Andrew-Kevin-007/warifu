## Known limits

- The Symbolon target runs the kernel-core build as merged: K-09, K-10, K-11, K-12, K-13, K-14, K-16 are fail-closed placeholders. A HOLD that exists only because of them is PENDING_CHECKS (inconclusive); no invoice can be released, so the auto-release and wrong-release gates are INCONCLUSIVE, not MET. They are meant to be re-run when the checks land (pnpm --filter @symbolon/attack-suite honest:unit).
- Every invoice is honest by construction and the corpus is generated: it measures false refusals on documents shaped like our own templates, not on real-world invoices. Real pilot invoices are the evidence for that (WP-27).
- Expected outcomes are written from docs/04, before and independent of any run. Where the spec itself is ambiguous the fixture keeps the reading of docs/07 §4 and the disagreement is logged in docs/QUESTIONS.md.
- Extraction reads are the fixtures' REFERENCE reads; no model was called, so the LLM cost is zero by construction and says nothing about a live model. Nothing here is labelled RECORDED.
- Engagements are fixture templates activated with placeholder signatures (SIMULATED), as in the attack matrix. Each invoice runs on a fresh scenario instance; there is no cross-invoice state beyond a fixture's own context patch.
- The parser block checks packages/extractor's deterministic readers (the zero-LLM path) against the same reference reads. It does not measure any LLM extractor; those figures are in packages/extractor/eval/results/extractor-eval.json and are SIMULATED.
- Latency is the wall-clock time of one in-process kernel-core evaluate() on the machine that wrote the file. It is not a service latency and not comparable across machines.
