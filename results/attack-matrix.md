## Known limits

- The Symbolon target runs the kernel-core build as merged: K-09, K-10, K-11, K-12, K-13, K-14, K-16 are fail-closed placeholders (WP-05 is not merged). Every intent ends HOLD, REJECT, ESCALATE or REPLAY; none can be released, so a count of 0 losses is not evidence about the missing checks.
- Engagements are the fixture templates activated with placeholder signatures and a placeholder registration (SIMULATED); no key signed anything and nothing was registered on a vault.
- Extraction reads are the fixture generator's REFERENCE reads, not model outputs; no model was called. B1's validator is a deterministic stand-in for an LLM validator (SIMULATED). Nothing in this report is labelled RECORDED because nothing was recorded.
- ATK-01, ATK-09, ATK-19, ATK-23 and ATK-24 have no oracle-expressible loss (docs/QUESTIONS WP-22 item 2). Their results are signal-scored or NOT_RUN and are counted separately from oracle-scored attacks.
- ATK-17 (contract calls), ATK-23 (approval rendering), the ATK-24 explainer check, the ATK-22 digest criteria and the fault steps need e2e-local, packages/receipts or K-12/K-15; the Symbolon target reports them NOT_RUN with the reason.
- Trials are extraction runs of the committed fixtures. Runs of one fixture share a payee, an amount and a document and are not independent, so the upper bounds are optimistic; the hold-out set (WP-36) is the independent evidence.
- Decisions accumulate across the steps of a fixture (docs/QUESTIONS WP-22 item 9). K-04 therefore sees earlier steps of the same fixture as near-duplicates or replays; see docs/QUESTIONS WP-23.
- B1 pays the face amount of the document and has no terms logic, so it overpays the early-pay discount on honest invoices; that is part of the pattern, and it is reported, not hidden.
- Every scenario instance is prefunded with the same 1,000,000 USDC; a transfer above the remaining balance reverts and moves no money, so losses are bounded by it.
