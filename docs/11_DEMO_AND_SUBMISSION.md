## 3. The `/judge` page (the reviewer's guided 5-minute path)

Single scrollable page with five numbered cards, each with a **button that works without login**. Served with the RECORDED fallback automatically (DEC-016).

1. **"Watch it get fooled and not lose money"**: runs ATK-10 on the sandbox in ~10 s: shows extractor output (fooled), kernel verdict `REJECT` with check IDs, B1/B0 outcome (loss), vault would revert.
2. **"Verify a real receipt yourself"**: link to a real mainnet/testnet receipt; `/audit/<hash>` recomputes hash, checks signature against the signer registered in the vault, and the on-chain `Released` anchor, **all client-side**.
3. **"Recompute the loss bound"** *(v2)*: certificate card — exclusions first, generated headline, status chip (VALID/STALE), horizon table, the copy-able `pnpm lbc verify <vault>` command, and the what-if sliders (same `bounds.ts`).
4. **"Try to break it"** *(v2)*: opens the challenge (different origin): one-click attacks, benign controls, write-your-own ≤ 4 KiB; scoreboard with "0 losses in N distinct human attempts (95 % upper bound ≈ 3/N)" — never "unbreakable".
5. **"See the real users"**: `/pilot` with explorer links per payment (pilot tenants only, INV-17).

Also show **"What this does not do"** (limitations: work quality, owner-key compromise, availability, unaudited code, simulated planner results) on the same page. Honesty is a credibility asset with this organizer.

## 5. Form answers (draft; Kevin finalizes with real numbers)

- **What did you build?** "Symbolon: an evidence-gated treasury kernel for AI agents that pay contractors in USDC on Arc. Don't trust the agent: the LLM extracts, proposes and plans (it also chooses when/how to pay, e.g. early-pay discounts, inside what the signed engagement allows); a deterministic kernel decides; an on-chain vault enforces payee binding, caps and human approval.
