## What did you build?
Warifu is a treasurer for AI agents that pay contractors in USDC on Arc, and it cannot be sweet-talked. The model reads invoices and proposes; a deterministic kernel decides; an on-chain vault enforces payee binding, caps and human approval. Don't trust the agent. Bound it. Prove it. Invite attack. Every decision carries a signed receipt anyone can recompute, and a public sandbox invites people to try to make it pay the wrong person.
